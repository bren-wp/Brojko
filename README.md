## Find your next high score

Swipe to merge matching tiles: 2 → 4 → 8 → 16 and beyond. Plan your next move, keep space on the board, and build your best score at your own pace.

## Make it yours

Choose light, dark or vivid themes and your favorite tile style. Track missions, achievements, records and game history. Resume your local game, adjust sound and vibration, and export or restore your local progress.

## Play in 30 languages

English is the default; choose another language from Settings. All language resources are included in the app and do not require an online translation service.
