Brojko is a colorful number-merging puzzle game you can enjoy at your own pace.

Swipe across the board to combine matching tiles and unlock bigger numbers. Reach 2048, beat your personal records and track your progress.

GAME MODES
• Classic 4×4: plan your moves and reach 2048.
• Relaxed 5×5: enjoy extra space for longer games.
• Daily challenge: reach 256 and 3,000 points within 80 moves.

WHY BROJKO?
• Colorful visuals and multiple themes.
• Stars for missions and daily challenges.
• Statistics, personal records and game history.
• Undo your last move.
• Play offline without an account.
• Save progress on your device and export a local backup.
• No ads or in-app purchases.

Merge numbers. Beat records.
